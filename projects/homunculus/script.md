# Voiceover

Susumu Nakoshi has lost almost everything.

He sleeps in his car and washes his suit in the park, clinging to the appearance of a life that has fallen apart.

A desperate bargain pulls him into a fever dream of the human psyche. People's fears and desires become grotesque, impossible to ignore.

He sees what others have spent a lifetime learning to look away from.

Shame. Cruelty. Desperation. The need to be loved.

And with every encounter, he is drawn further in, until looking deeper into other people becomes a descent into his own madness.
