# Motion and captions

Compare browser previews first. Keep choices in the session; do not render merely to demonstrate a style.

- Movement: still, slow or large. Choose per-panel crops and paths that preserve the focal point. Keep camera motion continuous when captions divide one image into several shots.
- Transitions: cut, crossfade or through black. Define transition duration and account for overlaps so the narration and total duration remain aligned.
- Captions: phrases, one spoken word, a phrase with the current word highlighted, or none. Word modes need word timings aligned to the selected recording, not evenly divided estimates.
- Keep captions as a separate composition layer. Review readable size, safe placement and contrast against each panel. Removing captions requires a new export.
- Picker previews record intent. Before production, check renderer support and implement any missing setting, then verify the resulting episode and frames. Keep existing exports unchanged during review.
