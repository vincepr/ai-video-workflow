# Organic social

Identify the platform, market, account owner and commercial relationship. A brand's own post, affiliate post and sponsored creator post may have different disclosure and eligibility rules. Check current community, commercial/branded-content and synthetic-media rules for the actual case. Organic distribution does not automatically permit a product excluded from paid advertising.

Plan a clear early hook, visible product and truthful benefit. Use the platform's current aspect ratio and interface safe areas. Add commercial and AI disclosures when applicable. Use music licensed for this account and commercial use, rather than assuming a trending sound is available to a brand.

Record the caption, CTA, destination link and applicable disclosures with the export. Publishing remains a separate action requiring a request. Boosting or Promote changes the use case to paid advertising.
