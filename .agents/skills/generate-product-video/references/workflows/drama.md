# Short drama

Plan a sequence with a clear conflict, cast roles and a resolution tied to verified product facts. Record environment and character references by role. Define each shot's location, continuity anchors, camera move, dialogue and transition before generating frames. Keep the chosen genre's tone without depicting invented product capabilities as part of the story.

Generate and inspect stages in order when a verified route exists; stitch only accepted shots. Preserve per-shot prompt files, reference hashes, raw outputs, timing plan and final review. The style catalog is creative configuration, not evidence that cast continuity, speech or multi-shot stitching is live-supported.
