# Shared product-video execution

Treat the frozen request and its copied inputs as the sole authority for generation. Inspect the selected product media and recorded facts before writing prompts. Preserve component count, material, markings, mechanism, dimensions and source axis order. Distinguish product measurements from packaging size; do not infer missing measurements or capabilities. Compare the first frame and full clip with the references, including scale and contact. Never infer a product claim from a visual effect, a hook or a setting. Any surreal treatment needs an ordinary-scale product view or clear creative framing so it cannot be mistaken for a measured feature.

Write actual image and motion prompt files in the run's work directory. Record each source path and hash, reference role, first-frame candidate and why it was selected or rejected. Plan the stages, then advance them automatically in dependency order unless the request selects a human checkpoint. Check model route, inputs, supported controls and the request's USD cap before a paid operation. Persist a submission receipt and job ID before polling; resume the same job after interruption. Stop with an explicit unsupported state when a requested operation has no verified route.

Keep raw provider files unchanged. Record stage decisions, inspection notes, an edit plan with frame-based trims and holds, local audio/caption sources, and pointers to raw output and numbered export. Review product identity, scale, contact, motion, language, audio, legibility and destination requirements separately. Record offline, live-provider and creative verdicts separately. Do not call a first-frame preview or an accepted job a finished video.
