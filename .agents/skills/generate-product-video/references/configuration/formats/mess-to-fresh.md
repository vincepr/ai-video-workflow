# Mess to Fresh

Open on a visibly messy area with the product identifiable. Make the signature beat a broad, satisfying change across the frame, such as clutter sweeping toward its proper places or a deliberate sequence of organizing actions. Land on the same angle with a clearly improved area and the same product. Filing one item is too small a payoff for this format. Keep the product's shape and existing contents stable; do not imply cleaning or another function it cannot perform.
