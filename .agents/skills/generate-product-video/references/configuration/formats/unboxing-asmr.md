# Unboxing ASMR

Open close on real packaging, hands and a tactile detail. Build the signature beat from successive, slow handling sounds and visible steps such as lifting a lid, peeling a seal and setting down an included part. End with a quiet full-product reveal. If the model cannot provide usable synchronized sound, plan recorded or licensed local Foley; silent macro motion alone is not an ASMR payoff. Omit narration, faces and generic selfie framing.
