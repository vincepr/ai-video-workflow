# Paid advertising

Check product eligibility for the requested platform and target market before spending on campaign production. Inspect the destination page as well as the creative. Age targeting, alcohol-free staging or softer wording does not automatically make an excluded product eligible.

Save the dated official policy source and the product-specific conclusion. If a paid placement excludes the product, report that limit while continuing authorized research or private creative work. Do not switch products or claim a workaround without the user choosing a different brief.

For eligible products, prepare hook variants, accurate offer/price, CTA, brand identity and platform-specific exports. Verify landing-page agreement, synthetic-media disclosures, music rights and required claims support. Record outstanding advertiser-account requirements. Creating an ad file does not authorize campaign setup, spending or launch.
