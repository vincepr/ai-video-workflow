# Shop and brand pages

Identify the merchant and exact destination, such as a Relaxdays product gallery, another shop, a brand social channel or a marketplace listing. Check that destination's current technical and content requirements. Brand social channels also use the organic or paid guide as appropriate.

Prioritize an accurate product demonstration, dimensions or included items that are verified, and clear detail views. Consider silent playback and captions. Produce vertical, square or landscape variants only for the requested placements; do not assume TikTok framing suits a product gallery.

Separate product facts from generated staging. Preserve the SKU's actual appearance and do not imply accessories, capacities or functions that are not included. Record merchant authorization for source assets and brand use, plus any synthetic-image labeling requirement. Providing a Relaxdays link does not establish a relationship with Relaxdays or access to its publishing systems.
