# Lessons from reviewed generation attempts

Read this before paying for new footage, along with the assigned project's saved reviews. Product-specific reports and media stay local when the user requests that boundary. Add transferable lessons here without copying product details into the skill.

## Creative value and spending

Identify what the generated clip contributes to the advertisement. A gentle camera move over a still product display may add little value and can often be produced locally. When the user explicitly wants a video-model test, camera-only motion can still be a useful experiment; label it as a test rather than evidence of a compelling advertisement. Do not silently replace requested generated video with animated stills.

Evaluate the hook and intended action before polishing atmosphere. Attractive lighting and an API returning a playable clip do not establish a useful result. If the user rejects the quality or value, record the attempt as failed even when generation completed technically. Stop further paid work until the next attempt has a specific improvement or a newly requested test.

Review a complete inexpensive ad draft using source images, temporary captions and sound before buying new footage. Assign each beat its purpose and medium. Use [workflow resources](workflow-resources.md) when designing that review or selecting reference and frame controls.

## Product layout and camera movement

Generating a complete multi-part bundle can introduce missing, merged or substituted items. Consider preserving the source arrangement with background editing or compositing, or using a focused detail shot when appropriate. Do not spend repeated requests fixing inventory without checking whether the shot itself improves the ad. Inspect the start image before video generation.

Leave room for every product element throughout the planned camera move. An object close to the starting-frame edge may be cropped during a push or slide. Evaluate ending composition as well as the starting image. If the framing cannot be verified, limit the move or widen the scene.

## Evidence and learning

Record provider errors separately from creative failures. Keep model/route capabilities, actual output size, unexpected audio and reported charges with the attempt. Account totals may lag individual receipts, so use recorded costs and outstanding request estimates when enforcing the budget.

Separate observations from inferred causes. A single rejected clip does not prove a model is universally poor. Save what the next attempt should change and what would count as improvement, then inspect the next result against those criteria. Preserve failed assets as evidence without marking them selected for a later advertisement.
